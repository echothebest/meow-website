Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Overview

Meow Client respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the Client and this website do (and do not) collect.

2. What We Do Not Collect

Meow Client does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your Minecraft username or account details.
  • Your IP address.
  • Your chat messages, worlds, or gameplay data.
  • Any telemetry, analytics, or usage statistics.

3. The Client Runs Locally

Everything Meow Client does happens on your own computer. The Client does not phone home or connect to any external server.

4. Config Files

Meow Client saves your settings to .minecraft/config/meow-client/config.json. This file stays on your computer.

5. This Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic access logs as part of serving any website. We do not have access to those logs.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. It uses sessionStorage only to remember whether you have seen the intro animation during the current session.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service this website uses is Google Fonts. Google may log the request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
