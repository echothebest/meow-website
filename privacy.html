Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Overview

Meow Client respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the Client and this website do (and do not) collect.

2. What We Do Not Collect

Meow Client does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your Minecraft username or account details.
  • Your IP address.
  • Your chat messages, worlds, or gameplay data.
  • Your hardware or system information.
  • Any telemetry, analytics, or usage statistics.

3. The Client Runs Locally

Everything Meow Client does happens on your own computer. The Client does not phone home, does not connect to any external server, and does not send any data anywhere.

4. Config Files

Meow Client saves your module settings to a local JSON file at:

.minecraft/config/meow-client/config.json

This file stays on your computer. It contains only your module toggles and settings — no personal information.

5. This Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic access logs (IP address, browser type) as part of serving any website. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. It uses your browser's sessionStorage only to remember whether you have already seen the intro animation during the current session. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service this website uses is Google Fonts, to load the fonts used on the site. Google may log the request. We do not control or access those logs.

8. Children's Privacy

Meow Client is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
